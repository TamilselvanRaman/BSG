# Client Demonstration Script: BSG Goods Management MVP

## Duration: 5–10 Minutes
## Target Audience: Mandi Traders, Commission Agents, Business Owners

### 1. Welcome & Dashboard (1.5 Mins)
- **Action**: Launch application and arrive on the Dashboard.
- **Narrative**:
  - *"Welcome to the BSG Buyer & Seller Goods Management System. The dashboard provides an instantaneous, real-time snapshot of your trading operations."*
  - *"Notice the 4 key financial metrics calculated authoritatively from actual records: Today's Active Sales, Initial Payments Received at unloading, Total Cash Collections, and Total Outstanding Buyer Dues across the market."*
  - *"Below, the 7-day volume chart distinguishes active sales from actual cash collected, ensuring you never mistake billed credit for received money."*

### 2. Customer Management & Ledger Audit (2 Mins)
- **Action**: Tap **Customers** navigation tab.
- **Narrative**:
  - *"Our Customer Directory handles Buyers, Sellers, and Dual-traders. Every card displays current outstanding dues in high-contrast Indian Rupees."*
  - Open customer profile (e.g., **Murugan Traders**).
  - Tap the **Account Ledger** tab.
  - *"Notice the complete double-entry ledger: each bill issued debits the buyer, each payment or advance credits the buyer, and a running balance is tracked line by line. The final balance matches the customer's outstanding balance with zero discrepancy."*

### 3. Goods Entry & Initial Payment (2 Mins)
- **Action**: Tap **New Goods Entry** quick action.
- **Narrative**:
  - *"When produce arrives at the mandi, we record basic info: Entry number, Lot number, vehicle number, and select the buyer."*
  - *"We support multiple product lines. Internal procurement cost rates are strictly separated from the selling rates charged to the buyer."*
  - Scroll to the highlighted green card: **Initial Payment Received from Buyer**.
  - *"A critical feature in produce mandis: often buyers pay a token advance or unloading deposit. We enter ₹5,000 via UPI right here. This advance is recorded in the customer's ledger immediately."*
  - Save entry and view details.

### 4. Billing, Invoice Preview & Initial Payment Deduction (2 Mins)
- **Action**: From the saved entry, tap **Generate Bill from This Entry**.
- **Narrative**:
  - *"When converting the lot into a formal bill, all products and subtotal are auto-populated."*
  - *"Look at the Live Billing Breakdown: Subtotal + Labour Charge = Current Bill Total. The buyer's previous outstanding dues are added. Crucially, the ₹5,000 initial advance is deducted exactly once from the gross amount payable, leaving the exact net remaining due."*
  - Tap **Save & Generate Bill**.
  - Review the professional **BSG** invoice layout.

### 5. WhatsApp Sharing (1 Min)
- **Action**: Tap **WhatsApp Share**.
- **Narrative**:
  - *"With one click, a cleanly formatted invoice summary is prepared for WhatsApp, complete with bill number, line items, advance deducted, remaining dues, and payment instructions. Tap 'Copy Message' or 'Open WhatsApp' for immediate delivery."*

### 6. Controlled Bill Cancellation (1 Min)
- **Action**: Open any bill and tap **Cancel Demo Bill**.
- **Narrative**:
  - *"In produce trading, weighbridge tare disputes or customer adjustments occasionally require cancelling a bill. Our system NEVER silently deletes financial records."*
  - Enter reason: *"Weighbridge tare mismatch (-45 kg)"* and confirm.
  - *"The bill is marked 'Cancelled' in bold red with full audit logs (reason, supervisor, timestamp). Active sales totals are reversed, and an auditable reversal credit is added to the customer ledger."*

### 7. Daily Summary & 8 Specialized Reports (1.5 Mins)
- **Action**: Navigate to **Daily Summary** and **Reports**.
- **Narrative**:
  - *"The Daily Summary allows date-by-date navigation with complete transaction breakdowns separating cash collections from credit sales."*
  - *"The Reports Hub provides 8 distinct reports: Daily Sales, Initial Payments, Collections, Outstanding Dues ranking, Customer Ledger, Goods Entries, Lot Numbers, and Cancelled Bills."*
  - *"Finally, in Settings, you can safely reset the demo records at any time with a confirmation prompt."*
